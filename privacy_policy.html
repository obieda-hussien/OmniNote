Privacy Policy

App: Omni Note+ · com.omni.noteplus
Developer: Abdelrahman Hussien (Omni)

Last Updated: October 11, 2026

Welcome to Omni Note+. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to protecting your data when you use our mobile application.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Core note editing and library management store your notes on your device. Optional online features can transmit the content needed for the requested operation.

Omni Note+ is an offline-first application. Notes, imported attachments, and application settings are stored in private app storage. Android backup and device transfer depend on your system settings.

2. Third-Party Services

The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not require an account to use local note features. We do not operate a server that stores your notes and we do not sell your personal data.

When a build is configured for Gemini refinement and you use it, the requested text is sent to Google's API. Speech recognition uses your chosen Android recognition service and may require a network connection. Remote media links can contact their destination providers.

3. Permissions

Microphone permission supports recording and dictation and is requested when those features need it. Attachments use the Android system file picker: you select specific files, and the app does not request broad access to your photo, video, audio or storage library. The app declares internet access for online refinement and remote media; Android does not show a runtime permission prompt for internet access.

4. Data Security

Note PINs restrict access through the app interface; they are not app-level encryption. The current database stores note content and note PINs locally without encryption. Device locks, system protections and backup settings affect the security of this data.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Local notes and settings remain on your device until you delete them or clear the app's storage. Moving a note to Trash does not permanently delete it; use the permanent-delete or empty-trash action to remove it from the note database. Imported attachment copies may remain in private app storage until that storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

The writing-history feature retains dates, counts and opaque record identities rather than deleted note text, titles, attachments or PINs. Clearing the app's storage removes its local history and settings. Files you exported or shared, and copies in Android backups, must be managed separately through their destination apps or your device/account backup settings.

Data sent to an optional external service is handled under that provider's policies. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy. This app does not offer account creation, so there is no app account to delete.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The current policy and its last-updated date will remain available on this page.
